Our Story

Every Dish
Has a Tale

Nestled in the Inner Market of Sector 7‑C, The Hedgehog Café is Chandigarh's most beloved literary‑themed dining destination. We're not just a café — we're a cozy haven where books line the walls, good music fills the air, and every dish on our menu is named after a literary character or tale.

Walk in, find a quiet corner, open a book — or just let the warm wood-panelled interiors and the scent of fresh coffee slow you down. Freelancers, writers, families, friends: everyone finds their chapter here.

Our kitchen crafts everything from a hearty English Breakfast and Peter Pan Cakes to Alfredo In Wonderland pasta and Gone Grill — all with the same literary passion that defines our space.
